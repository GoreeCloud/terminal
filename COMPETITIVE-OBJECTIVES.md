# Competitive Objectives

GoreeCloud Terminal should be evaluated against mature terminals such as GNOME Terminal, Konsole, Kitty, WezTerm, and Alacritty without copying their identities or assuming one architecture is mandatory.

Primary differentiation objectives are visible administrative context, local/remote/container safety, long-running infrastructure sessions, portable profiles, shell-aware boundaries, recovery/reconnection, accessibility, large-log responsiveness, privacy-first operation, GoreeCloud platform integration, and polished Glaze UI presentation.

Performance or superiority claims require measured evidence.
